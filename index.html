What does “temperature anomaly” mean?

It is the difference from the 1951–1980 average for the same place and time of year. A value of +1°C means that period was one degree warmer than its reference average; it is not the absolute air temperature.

HOW TO READ THISEvidence, not prediction

Monthly observations are averaged into annual values to reduce the seasonal cycle. Sen slope estimates a straight-line change per decade. Mann–Kendall tests for a consistent direction; its lag-1 adjustment is approximate. A non-significant result is still a finding: variability may be high or the record may be too short. This first version does not model curved trends or abrupt shifts.
